Every AI engineering workshop starts the same way. Someone is missing an API key. Someone has the wrong Node version. Someone's laptop refuses to run Docker. The first hour goes to setup. The work starts after lunch.

We built [**Open Mercato Sandboxes** t](https://openmercatocloud.com/)o delete that hour. Today they are generally available.

## What a Sandbox is

A computer in your browser, billed per minute of use. Inside: every tool you need for AI engineering, already wired together.

- **Coding agents:** Codex and Claude, ready on first login.
- **Editor:** VS Code in the browser, with diff view and logs.
- **Git:** commit, branch, export to GitHub.
- **Preview and sharing:** a public or password-protected link to your running app.
- **Any stack:** TypeScript, Python, .NET, Go. Bring your own runtime.
- **App templates:** pre-configured sandboxes for specific applications, for example Open Mercato CRM.

You sign up, get free credits for hardware and AI, and start working in under 30 seconds. No install. No config. No "works on my machine".

Some of us stopped carrying an open laptop everywhere. The agent runs in the Sandbox 24/7. You check progress from your phone, review the diff, accept or reject. The machine keeps going.

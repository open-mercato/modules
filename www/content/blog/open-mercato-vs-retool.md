## Two Different Philosophies

Retool and Open Mercato solve related but fundamentally different problems. Retool is a mature, polished low-code platform for building internal tools quickly via drag-and-drop. Open Mercato is an open-source code-first framework for teams that want to build CRM/ERP systems with full code ownership.

Neither is universally "better" - the right choice depends on your team, your use case, and your priorities.

## The Bigger Question

The real decision isn't "which tool is better", it's "what kind of application are you building?"

If you're building internal tools on top of existing databases and APIs, a low-code platform like Retool gets you there fast.

If you're building a business-critical CRM/ERP system where you need full control, architectural consistency, and the ability to scale your engineering team with AI, a code-first foundation framework like Open Mercato is designed for that.

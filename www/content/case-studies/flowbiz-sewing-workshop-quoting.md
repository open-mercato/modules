## What does a second print colour cost?

Two customers could order the same canvas bag and need different prices. One might want a print on both sides. The other might order enough bags for a different cutting layout to save a roll of fabric. The experienced estimators knew which details mattered. Working out their combined effect meant opening a dozen spreadsheets, often with different versions of the same fabric price.

Flowbiz founder Mateusz Kozłowski spent six months building a first version in Retool. It handled simpler orders. The difficult ones exposed how much work each change required: someone had to click through combinations of fabrics, pieces and quantities to check that an edit hadn't broken another price. Flowbiz moved the configurator onto Open Mercato.

## A quote starts with the pieces on the cutting table

Sales opens a quote, picks the customer and adds a bag. The product supplies the expected dimensions and pattern pieces. The estimator chooses a fabric and colour for each piece, specifies the print, and adds straps, labels or a zip where needed. A different front and back can live on the same quote line.

Quantity and cutting layout belong in the same calculation as labour, add-ons and margin. Flowbiz gave the workshop four places to maintain those inputs: fabrics, pattern pieces, extras and pricing rules. Changing a fabric price once feeds the new figure into future quotes. Sales can still open a line and see how the price was calculated.

## The next order can start from the last quote

Open Mercato already handled customer records, sales documents, revisions and conversion to an order. That left Flowbiz to build the part peculiar to a sewing workshop. In the walkthrough, Kozłowski also asks the assistant to find an existing quote and duplicate it. The assistant asks for confirmation before making the copy.

Flowbiz reports that a quote that used to take around 40 minutes now takes 5-10. The quickest result applies to a narrower case: when a customer wants a small variation on an archived quote, duplicating and adjusting it can take 1-2 minutes. The estimator can spend that time checking what the customer changed.

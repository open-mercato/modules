## A 240 m² house and two prices to return

The homeowner wanted to know what it would cost to skim coat the walls and ceilings, and what it would cost to paint them. The email described a three-storey house of about 240 m² in Warsaw and included the architect’s drawings. It also asked a question a lump sum would leave unanswered. How many coats of paint were included?

The renovation company owner who approached [Evojam](https://evojam.com/) prepared every estimate himself. He told the team that a small job took four to five hours to price and that a larger one could take two to three days. Up to ten inquiries could land on his desk in a week.

Evojam built Fachura on Open Mercato and ran the Warsaw request through it. The estimator opened the email in AI Inbox, read the proposed action and accepted it. Fachura opened a deal in the CRM and passed the brief and drawings to the estimating workflow.

## What a square metre of painting buys

Fachura reads the scope, works out quantities from the plans and matches each finish to a service in the contractor’s catalog. Evojam loaded 45 renovation services into Open Mercato. Each carries its own unit and rate. Painting is priced by the square metre. Installing an interior door is priced per door. If the units don’t match, Fachura flags the line and leaves it out of the draft.

For the Warsaw job, the catalog supplied a detail the homeowner had specifically asked for. The standard painting service covered **two coats after surface preparation**. The estimator could open that record and explain what the price included.

## The two lines behind PLN 8,353.50

The draft priced **111.38 m² for each finish**. A single gypsum skim coat at PLN 35 per square metre came to PLN 3,898.30. Painting at PLN 40 per square metre came to PLN 4,455.20. Together, the two lines totalled **PLN 8,353.50 including tax**. The homeowner could see what each part of the requested work would cost.

The estimator could also see where the calculation needed judgment. One room had a printed ceiling height of 2.74 metres, but Fachura had inferred several wall lengths from the proportions of the drawing. It recorded that distinction on the quote line. The estimator now had specific walls to check at a site visit.

## What the estimator does with the first draft

Evojam reports that the Warsaw request reached a priced draft in minutes. The deal stops at _Do sprawdzenia_, or “Ready for review”. The estimator can check the assumptions, change a line and send the offer from Open Mercato. In the recording, the quote changes to _Sent_ after the operator sends it. The original request and the quote stay linked to the same deal for the next customer call.

## About

FilaNovum is a technology strategy and engineering firm positioning itself around full IP ownership and operational sovereignty - clients own the codebase and infrastructure outright rather than renting a SaaS. Built on Open Mercato as its core foundation.

## What we do

Strategic consulting - architecture and AI strategy - followed by Proof of Concept sprints, MVP builds, full-scale implementation, and managed stewardship after launch. FilaNovum also ships its own product, ProposalPrime, an AI-powered proposal management tool.

## Our specializations

- Proof of Concept Sprint - 3 weeks, fixed scope
- High-Fidelity MVP - 8-12 weeks
- Full-Scale Implementation and managed stewardship

## Who we work with

Companies in Industry 4.0, MedTech/healthcare, and B2B e-commerce that want to own their technology outright instead of building on a vendor's platform.

## Industries we serve

Industry 4.0, MedTech and healthcare, and B2B e-commerce.

## How we work

Fixed-scope engagements sized to the stage: Proof of Concept ($8,000, 3 weeks), MVP ($25,000, 8-12 weeks), Full-Scale Implementation ($75,000+).

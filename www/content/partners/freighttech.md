## About

FreightTech (Gdynia, Poland) builds an open global trade logistics operating ecosystem on Open Mercato modules. Led by Sebastian Wróbel (General Lead, 20 years in shipping) and Wojciech Bakłazec (Tech Lead), with Weronika Rochacka Gagliardi (Service Design Lead) and 15+ contributors from Hapag Lloyd, LPP Logistics, Port of Gdańsk Authority, and ROHLIG SUUS Logistics.

## What we do

An Air & Ocean Operating System - capacity planning, shipment orchestration, delay prediction, and exception management, with AI-powered document processing, rate comparison, and shipment matching. Modules for booking, scheduling, procurement, and document handling that forwarders and vendors can assemble, adapt, and extend themselves.

## Our specializations

- Open-source freight tech infrastructure on Open Mercato
- AI-powered document processing and shipment matching
- Exception handling automation
- Custom tool development for forwarders and carriers

## Who we work with

Freight forwarders, carriers, and logistics operators who want to own their technology and data instead of renting a closed platform.

## Industries we serve

Global freight logistics, air and ocean shipping, customs agencies, logistics service providers, and carrier operations.

## How we work

Built as an open industry initiative, not a single vendor - contributors from major logistics players sit inside the build, not just wait for delivery. Real-time operations data exchange replaces systems that don't talk to each other.

## About

TraceCore is an MRP/ERP system for food and supplement production under GMP compliance, built by BGW Pharma (Połchowo, Poland) and tested on its own premium supplement production line before being rewritten on Open Mercato for the wider market.

## What we do

LOT/batch traceability, production planning with built-in GMP forms, inventory and warehouse management with expiry tracking, procurement with mandatory unloading protocols, recipe versioning, audit trail and IQ/OQ/PQ preparation, an AI assistant, IoT hall-parameter monitoring, and multi-site/API integrations.

## Our specializations

- Full LOT traceability from raw material intake to finished goods
- GMP audit documentation - first batch live on day 5, audit-ready production in 12 weeks
- On-premise or dedicated VPS deployment - full data ownership, no vendor lock-in

## Who we work with

Food and supplement manufacturers and contract manufacturers (CDMO) operating under GMP who need audit-ready traceability without an 18-month SAP project.

## Industries we serve

Food production, supplement manufacturing, and contract manufacturing (CDMO).

## How we work

Paid Proof of Concept (€12,000-15,000, credited toward implementation), core implementation (€55,000-65,000, one-time, 12 months support included), and an optional annual service agreement (€12,000-14,000/year).

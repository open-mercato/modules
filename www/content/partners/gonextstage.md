## About

GoNextStage builds business applications for companies seeking a higher level of operational efficiency and process control. We connect processes, people and data across the enterprise. Companies like BNP Paribas Real Estate, VINCI Construction or Canal+ Poland choose GoNextStage because we combine Proven Business Logic, a holistic understanding of complex operations, and ~10 years of experience in turning business needs into practical technology solutions based on AI-native platforms.

## What we do

- Scalable and flexible business applications for better workflow
- Custom integrations connecting data silos (ERP ↔ BPM, BIM ↔ BPM, KSeF ↔ ERP)
- Analysis and automation of processes
- End-to-end support in digital transformation of organizations

## Industries we serve

GoNextStage has extensive experience delivering process automation and business applications for construction, real estate, and property management organizations, where operations span multiple stakeholders, projects, budgets, and compliance requirements. Among GoNextStage's clients are VINCI Construction Poland, Canal+ Poland, BNP Paribas Real Estate, Budimex and LCP.

Beyond construction and real estate, we also support organizations in financial services, shared service centers, manufacturing, and professional services.

## How we work

We work with Proven Business Logic (PBL): reusable business components built on our project experience. This allows us to launch solutions faster without reinventing common workflows, reduce implementation risk and validate the solution in a pilot before full production rollout. For client-specific applications, we develop the solution in stages, with regular validation, progress reviews, and room to refine requirements as the project evolves, helping minimize risk along the way.

Our goal is simple: to help your organization operate at a higher level.

## About

Nardeo (Poland) designs and implements dedicated software systems and automation, built around the idea that technology should be a stable foundation rather than a generic off-the-shelf template.

## What we do

Dedicated web applications and business systems, technical infrastructure and maintenance automation, business logic and data-flow architecture, and AI and language-model-based automation for repetitive work. Also runs a dedicated product line for the agriculture sector.

## Our specializations

- Dedicated systems built around the client's actual process rather than a fixed template
- Automated technical maintenance so new features ship predictably
- AI and language-model-based automation for repetitive tasks

## Who we work with

Companies that have outgrown generic off-the-shelf software and need a system designed around how they actually operate.

## Industries we serve

Agriculture, with general application across industries needing custom process and application development.

## How we work

Regular check-ins throughout the project rather than a long silent build phase, with delivery typically starting within one to two weeks of engagement.

## About

Commerce Weavers (Łódź, Poland) is an engineering-focused e-commerce agency built on Sylius and Symfony, known for architecting scalable B2B/B2C platforms, rescuing stuck implementations, and mentoring client dev teams. The team joined the Open Mercato ecosystem as a formal partner in May 2026.

## What we do

Custom e-commerce implementations and migrations, project rescue and optimization of existing systems, technical audits and training, PoC development, product configurators and backend integrations, B2B platforms and multi-vendor marketplaces. Open Mercato extends that into the ERP, CRM, and business applications that run alongside the store - less time on foundations, more room for the client's actual processes.

## Our specializations

- E-commerce project rescue - resolving architectural bottlenecks in existing Sylius/Symfony builds
- B2B platforms and multi-vendor marketplaces with product configurators
- High-performance builds for specialized sectors: agriculture, online printing, industrial component distribution

## Who we work with

Teams running B2B or B2C e-commerce on Sylius/Symfony who need architecture that scales without vendor lock-in, plus companies wanting an ERP/CRM layer next to their store built the same way.

## Industries we serve

Agriculture, online printing, industrial component distribution, and general B2B/B2C e-commerce.

## How we work

No vendor lock-in, no unnecessary complexity - Sylius stays the core e-commerce framework, Open Mercato is the complementary extension for what sits around the store.

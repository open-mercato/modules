## About

300.codes (Warsaw, Poland) is a software house built around marketing and sales outcomes - organic traffic growth, lead cost reduction, and conversion - backed by 13+ years of team experience and clients including BNP Paribas, Roche, PwC, and Swiss Krono.

## What we do

Custom e-commerce, web and mobile applications, content portals, e-learning platforms (LMS), ERP/CRM systems on Open Mercato, and UI/UX design. Stack spans Vue.js, React, and Astro on the front end, Symfony, Laravel, and Python on the back end.

## Our specializations

- Pharma e-commerce - Melisa.pl and Wapteka.pl e-pharmacy platforms
- Gamified e-learning for physicians (Dr Sim)
- ERP/CRM builds on Open Mercato

## Who we work with

Pharma, fintech, and healthcare companies that need both engineering execution and a marketing-and-sales lens on the result.

## Industries we serve

Pharma, fintech, e-commerce, and healthcare.

## How we work

Award-winning delivery (Golden Arrow, Złote Spinacze, Young Creatives, Dubai Lynx, Deloitte Gazele Biznesu) with Google, Meta, and SAR partnerships.

## About

ScaleOMa is the Open Mercato practice of [Itrix](https://itrix.pl), a Polish CRM/ERP implementation agency. Itrix's core team of 10+ analysts, implementers, and consultants has delivered 362+ CRM and automation projects.

## What we do

We configure Open Mercato around the client's existing processes instead of forcing a template fit, ship a first implementation on a fixed price within 3-6 weeks, and stay on with dedicated support as the system grows.

## Our specializations

- Open Mercato implementation and configuration
- CRM setup and optimization
- Business process automation with AI
- Integrations with e-commerce, accounting, warehouse, and shipping platforms

## Who we work with

Growing companies that want out of spreadsheets and disconnected tools without the multi-month, multi-vendor rollout that usually comes with it.

## Industries we serve

E-commerce and retail operations are our Open Mercato focus. Through Itrix, the team also carries deep experience in construction, manufacturing, renewable energy, real estate, tourism, and franchise networks.

## How we work

Fixed price, no hourly billing. You talk directly to the person implementing your system, not an account manager. No vendor lock-in - the platform is open source.

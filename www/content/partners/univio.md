## About

Univio is a technology partner for companies building competitive advantage in digital commerce. We combine the efficiency of day-to-day business operations with the development of the next stages of growth and innovation - and we have been doing it since 1997. Digital Expertise. Delivered.

## What we do

We support organisations across four areas: Digital Consulting, Commerce and Experience Solutions, AI, Data and Cloud Services, and Experience Optimization. We work end-to-end - from strategy and implementation to continuous development and optimisation.

## Our specializations

We have been exploring the world of new technologies for years and bringing our clients into it - from the first e-commerce systems to today's AI-powered solutions. We use AI in designing and developing systems in an AI-native way, accelerating business outcomes while keeping AI under the control of experienced teams and an AI harness.

## Who we work with

We work with market leaders - including RTV EURO AGD, Wittchen, Agata Meble, Tubądzin, mBank, Gemini Polska, iSpot, Super-Pharm and LuxMed. We build long-term relationships - with many of our clients we have been working together for over 10 years. Based on experience from over 500 projects, we serve 150+ satisfied clients.

## Industries we serve

We have deep knowledge of industries such as retail, FMCG, construction, home and decor, healthcare and finance - in both B2C and B2B models, as well as in the digitalisation of manufacturer processes. We combine a business and technology perspective, tailoring solutions to the specifics of each industry and the concrete challenges of each organisation.

## How we work

We adapt our model of cooperation to the needs of each client - from full end-to-end project delivery to supporting internal IT teams, or a hybrid model including full project handover to the client. We work on large-scale, multi-stage projects with complex dependencies and integrations - and we do it in a structured, predictable way.

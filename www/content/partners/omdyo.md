## About

Omdyo is a Polish studio built around Oliwia (Agentic Designer, UI/UX) and Maciej (.NET Engineer, Solution Architect) - both part of the Open Mercato core team, with 12+ and 13+ years respectively across logistics, retail, e-commerce, hospitality, and banking systems.

## What we do

Omdyo builds on Open Mercato where it fits - auth, RBAC, multi-tenancy, audit log, and custom entities already solved - and spends the project on the business-specific 20%: workflows, approvals, rules, UX/UI, portals, and role-based experiences. Not every project gets forced into one framework; the architecture follows the problem - extend Open Mercato, integrate an existing stack, or build dedicated.

## Our specializations

- Manufacturing workflow systems - production orders, operator panels, quality control, material flow, ERP/MES/WMS integration
- B2B commerce and self-service portals - orders, product catalogs, pricing
- Mobile apps for operational teams - warehouse, service, delivery, sales

## Who we work with

Companies that have outgrown off-the-shelf CRM/ERP/WMS/commerce tools and need the last 20% - the exceptions, integrations, and user experience - built around how they actually operate.

## Industries we serve

Manufacturing and production, e-commerce, logistics, service operations, and retail.

## How we work

Stack: .NET services, React/Next.js, React Native, .NET MAUI, with AI-assisted delivery under code review, security, and quality control.

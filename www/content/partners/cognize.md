## About

Cognize (Katowice, Poland) is the e-commerce practice of Grupa KMK, delivering B2B and B2C commerce, PIM, and AI-agent systems for manufacturers and distributors. Forbes Diamond Award winner in 2026.

## What we do

Platform implementations on Magento 2 and Sellina (its own B2B/B2C platform), PIM implementations on Akeneo and Ergonode, dedicated systems, consulting, UX/UI design, and integrations with ERP/OMS platforms including Comarch, SAP, and Streamsoft.

## Our specializations

- B2B/B2C e-commerce on Magento 2 and Sellina
- PIM implementation on Akeneo and Ergonode
- AI agents integrated into commerce and PIM workflows

## Who we work with

Manufacturers and distributors running B2B and B2C sales who need commerce, product data, and ERP/OMS systems working together.

## Industries we serve

Manufacturing, distribution, electrical and lighting, industrial automation, healthcare, agriculture, printing, heating technology, office supplies, sports, and automotive.

## How we work

Headless and PWA architecture where needed, backed by custom ERP/OMS integrations and a Forbes Diamond Award for business growth.

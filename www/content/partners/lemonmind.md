## About

LemonMind (Gdańsk, Poland) has run 70+ PIM and e-commerce implementations since 2008, with a 30+ person team of product-data and e-commerce specialists. Built on Pimcore, Akeneo, Ergonode, OroCommerce, Shopware, and Medusa, plus its own AI layer, LemonHub.AI.

## What we do

PIM/DAM/MDM implementations, B2B and B2C e-commerce platforms, product configurators, ETL data transformation, ETIM/BMECat classification, Pimcore training and audits, and AI-powered product-data solutions.

## Our specializations

- PIM/DAM/MDM on Pimcore, Akeneo, Ergonode
- B2B/B2C e-commerce on OroCommerce, Shopware, Medusa
- AI-driven product data and content automation (LemonHub.AI)

## Who we work with

Manufacturers, distributors, and retailers managing large or messy product catalogs across health/beauty, building/HVAC/electrical, and home furnishings.

## Industries we serve

Health, beauty and lifestyle; building, HVAC, and electrical; home furnishings; manufacturing and distribution.

## How we work

A multi-agent architecture builds automated data pipelines with an orchestrator. The team is integrating with Open Mercato to extend product-data work into the CRM and logistics layer next to it.

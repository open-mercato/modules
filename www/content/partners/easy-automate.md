## About

Easy Automate (Łódź, Poland) is a process-automation agency founded by Damian Stańczyk, focused on traditional-industry SMBs that are new to automating their operations.

## What we do

Free process audits, business process automation, AI department implementation, chatbots and conversational systems, CRM integration (including ActiveCampaign), data synchronization between existing tools, and form and reporting automation.

## Our specializations

- Process automation for companies up to 50 employees
- AI department implementation for teams without in-house AI capacity
- CRM and tool integration without replacing existing systems

## Who we work with

Small and mid-sized businesses across logistics, hospitality, manufacturing, quality control, marketing, and HR that want automation results without a long implementation project.

## Industries we serve

Logistics, hospitality, manufacturing, quality control, marketing, and HR.

## How we work

Measurable results targeted within two weeks, integrated with the client's existing tools rather than replacing them.

## About

10Clouds (Warsaw, Poland) is an AI development and consulting company working with financial institutions. The Open Mercato partner entity is 10Clouds Financial Institutions Sp. z o.o., led by CEO Karol Stępień. Rated 4.9/5.0 by 90+ clients on Clutch.

## What we do

AI-powered operations for banks, payment providers, insurers, factoring and leasing companies: AIConsole, an enterprise platform for orchestrating AI agents and workflows across enterprise systems; Company GPT, secure internal assistants built on enterprise data; and AI implementation covering integrations, governance, security, and compliance.

## Our specializations

- AI agent orchestration (AIConsole platform)
- Automation of business workflows in operations, finance, and customer processes
- Secure internal AI assistants on enterprise data (Company GPT)
- Agentic commerce for retail and e-commerce, including MCP integrations and conversational discovery
- Enterprise blockchain, smart contracts, and DeFi platforms

## Who we work with

Enterprises and scaleups in regulated financial environments. Public references include PZU, Samsara, Forbes, ETS, Asmodee, Qenta, Trust Stamp, and Aleph Zero.

## Industries we serve

Banking, payments, insurance, factoring, leasing, fintech, and e-commerce.

## How we work

Product delivery from idea to launch with an agile playbook, plus an AI Labs track for testing AI against concrete business problems. Client intellectual property remains the client's; work runs under non-disclosure agreements and documented data-handling procedures.

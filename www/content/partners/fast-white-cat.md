## About

Fast White Cat (Wrocław, Poland) is an e-commerce development agency with nearly 15 years building and optimizing online sales platforms, processing up to 300,000 orders a month for its clients. Official Adobe Partner, certified Magento developers, AWS certified, and SAP certified partner.

## What we do

B2B and B2C e-commerce implementations on Magento 2 and Shopify, omnichannel platforms, headless and PWA builds, AI integration, technology consulting, and ongoing platform maintenance and optimization.

## Our specializations

- Magento 2 development - certified Adobe Commerce partner
- Shopify implementations
- Omnichannel, headless, and PWA e-commerce architecture

## Who we work with

Retailers and manufacturers running high-volume B2B/B2C commerce who need a partner past go-live, not just a build-and-leave vendor.

## Industries we serve

Retail and e-commerce, with clients spanning manufacturing, toys, leasing, and cosmetics - including FAKRO, Coccodrillo, and Tolpa.

## How we work

Adobe, AWS, and SAP certified - technical credibility from vendor certifications, paired with strategic advice focused on sales results, not just functionality.

## About

Enxoo (Warsaw, Poland, with international offices) is a Salesforce-focused digital transformation and cloud consultancy for telecommunications and enterprise operators - 200+ experts holding 700+ Salesforce certifications, led by Chief Commercial Officer Ewa Jaśkowiak.

## What we do

Salesforce implementations and integrations that turn next-generation telco strategy into operational impact - fiber-based ISP solutions, wholesale carrier automation, data center connectivity and billing, enterprise B2B workflows, AI and MuleSoft integration, and wealth management operations.

## Our specializations

- Telecom operator automation - fiber ISP, wholesale carrier, data center billing
- Salesforce implementation and integration at enterprise scale
- AI and MuleSoft integration for complex B2B workflows

## Who we work with

Telecom operators and enterprise B2B organizations reducing operational complexity, plus financial services firms running wealth management operations.

## Industries we serve

Telecommunications, financial services, retail, and healthcare.

## How we work

Enterprise-grade Salesforce delivery backed by a 200+ person certified team, working with operators including Deutsche Telekom, Tele2, and Rossmann.

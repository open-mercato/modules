## About

AMT Solution (Wrocław, Poland) is a remote-first TYPO3, Symfony, and PHP development studio operating since 2011, working with agencies and direct clients across Europe.

## What we do

Custom development on PHP, Symfony, Laravel, and TYPO3 CMS, plus subcontracted development capacity for agencies that need a dependable PHP partner.

## Our specializations

- TYPO3 CMS development and maintenance
- Symfony and Laravel backend development
- Open Mercato core contribution - merged pull requests spanning sales, customer management, RBAC, and CLI tooling

## Who we work with

Agencies that need a PHP and TYPO3 subcontractor, and direct clients building on the same stack.

## Industries we serve

General B2B clients across the PHP and TYPO3 ecosystem.

## How we work

Remote-first delivery for agencies and direct clients across Europe.

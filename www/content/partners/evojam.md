## About

Evojam (Warsaw, Poland) is a software development studio founded in 2014, built around AI-driven consulting and custom web and mobile product development, with a 4.9/5 Clutch rating.

## What we do

Custom software development, AI and automation consulting, UX/UI design, product discovery workshops, code and architecture audits, testing and QA, and ERP/CRM builds on Open Mercato. Evojam also runs a Design Venture Lab and PropTech-specific work - ESG workshops, digital twins, legacy system refactoring.

## Our specializations

- ERP/CRM solutions on Open Mercato
- PropTech - ESG workshops, digital twins, legacy system refactoring
- AI and automation consulting alongside custom web/mobile development

## Who we work with

Pragmatic innovators and startups building digital products that solve real problems, plus real estate and PropTech companies modernizing legacy systems.

## Industries we serve

Real estate (PropTech), tech and SaaS, and startups.

Agile delivery from discovery through deployment with continuous stakeholder engagement - transparency and open communication over a black-box handoff.
